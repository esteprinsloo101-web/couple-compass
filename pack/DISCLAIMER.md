# Couple Compass — Hard Disclaimer

**Read this before using any script or worksheet.**

## What this is
Couple Compass is a **faceless, general-purpose relationship self-help toolkit** for consenting adults (18+). It offers scenario cards, conversation scripts, and printable worksheets based on widely known psychology *frameworks* (educational only).

## What this is NOT
- **Not** couples therapy, counselling, psychotherapy, or clinical advice  
- **Not** mediation, legal advice, or conflict resolution services  
- **Not** diagnosis or treatment of any mental-health condition  
- **Not** an AI therapist or chatbot counsellor  
- **Not** a substitute for a registered psychologist, counsellor, social worker, GP, or emergency care  
- **Not** for people under 18  

Couple Compass / the seller is **not** a clinic or HPCSA practice.

## Domestic violence & safety
**Do not use this pack if you are in active domestic violence, coercive control, or fear for your safety.**

If you are unsafe, threatened, controlled, or hurt:
1. Leave the materials and get to safety if you can.  
2. Call emergency services: **112** (South Africa).  
3. SAPS: **10111**.  
4. GBV Command Centre: **0800 428 428** (24h) or from a cellphone **\*120\*7867#**.  
5. More local helplines: [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

These tools are for ordinary friction between willing partners — **not** for staying in or “repairing” an abusive relationship.
