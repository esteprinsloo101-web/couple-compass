# Script — Appreciation habit

**Not therapy.** Adults 18+ only.

## Rule
Specific behaviour + impact. No “but…”.

## Template
> “I noticed you ___[specific action]___. It helped me feel ___[impact]___. Thank you.”

## Three rounds (write then speak)
1. Something that made life easier.  
2. A quality under pressure.  
3. A small moment you almost missed.

## Mid-week boost (optional text)
> “Quick thanks — I appreciated ___ today.”

## Pause if
Appreciation is being used to paper over harm or control — stop and get outside help.
