# Script — Conflict repair

**Not therapy or mediation.** Not for active abuse. Adults 18+ only.

## Cool-down first
If voices or heart rates are high: agree a **20-minute break** and a return time.
> “I’m flooded. Let’s pause until ___:___ and come back.”

## Soft start-up (each)
> “When ___[situation]___, I felt ___[feeling]___. I need ___[clear ask]___.”

## Ownership (even 10%)
> “I got heated. I’m sorry for ___[specific behaviour]___. I want to understand your side.”

## Reconnect ask
> “What would help you feel a little safer with me right now?”

## Tiny repair action
Pick one: water · short walk · apology text · redo a chore · sit side-by-side quietly 5 min.

## STOP — domestic violence
If there is violence, threats, intimidation, or fear: **do not use this script to stay**.  
Call **112** · SAPS **10111** · GBV **0800 428 428** · findahelpline.com
