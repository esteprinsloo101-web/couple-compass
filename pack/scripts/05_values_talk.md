# Script — Values talk

**Not therapy or mediation.** Adults 18+ only. Curiosity over verdicts.

## Step 1 — Each pick top 3 values
Examples: honesty · kindness · faith · family · ambition · rest · fairness · adventure · security · growth · community · creativity (or invent your own).

## Step 2 — Share a story
> “One value that feels loud for me is ___ because ___.”

## Step 3 — Map overlap & difference
> “I see we both care about ___. Where we differ on ___, I want to stay curious.”

## Step 4 — One weekly behaviour
> “This week, can we try ___ that supports our shared value of ___?”

## Red flags — stop
Ultimatums, isolation from support, control of money/identity → outside help.
