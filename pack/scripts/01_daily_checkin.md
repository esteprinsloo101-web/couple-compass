# Script — Daily check-in (5 minutes)

**Not therapy.** Adults 18+ only. Skip if unsafe.

## Setup
- Phones away. Timer optional (5 min).
- Speaker finishes; listener reflects one sentence; then swap.

## Round (each person)
1. **High:** One good moment today / yesterday.  
2. **Low:** One friction or disappointment.  
3. **Need:** One clear wish for the next 24 hours.

## Listener line
> “I heard that your high was ___, your low was ___, and you need ___. Did I get that right?”

## Soft close
> “Thanks for sharing. One thing I can do to support your need is ___.”

## Pause if
Either partner feels unsafe, mocked, or flooded → stop. Safety: 112 · GBV 0800 428 428.
